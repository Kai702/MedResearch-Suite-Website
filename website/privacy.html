Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 November 2026

In short. MedResearch Suite runs on your own computer. The data you enter or import, your analyses and your results stay on that computer. The app has no account, no cloud storage, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting, and the developer never receives the data you work with.

A few features that you choose to use reach services on the internet. Each is listed below, with exactly what it sends and to whom.

Who Makes MedResearch Suite

MedResearch Suite is made and published by HariOm S. Vaja, an individual developer. For any question about privacy, or to ask that something you sent be deleted, write to support@medresearchsuite.com.

The Data You Work With

Datasets, patient-level data, study data and everything you type into the analysis workspaces are processed by the app's statistics engine on your own computer. The engine accepts connections from that computer only. Your data is held in memory while the app is open and is not written to disk, except in files you choose to save or export. It is never sent to the developer or to anyone else, and the analyses work with no internet connection at all.

Before any text you type can leave your computer through one of the online features below, the app checks it on your computer for patient identifiers, such as names, dates or record numbers. If something looks like one, nothing is sent unless you confirm twice, and the result then carries a short note that you confirmed the text identifies no patient.

What Leaves Your Computer, and When

Only the following, and only when you use the feature named:

FeatureWhat is sentTo whom
Literature search (Discovery) Your search words; the PubMed identifiers of the papers found; the email address and NCBI key you enter in Preferences, if any (NCBI asks programs to send an email address with their searches) The U.S. National Library of Medicine: PubMed and LitSense (NCBI), ClinicalTrials.gov, and NIH iCite
AI suggestions (optional) Your search words and the abstracts of the papers found, with your own key for the provider you chose Google (Gemini), OpenAI or Anthropic, under your own account and that provider's terms
Feedback The message you type and, if you give one, your email address Web3Forms, a form delivery service, which passes it to the developer's mailbox
Update check Two requests for fixed files, about once a day, carrying nothing about you or your data GitHub, where the app's releases are published

Each of these services receives your computer's network address, as any website you visit does, and its own privacy policy applies to what it receives. Your API keys stay on your computer, encrypted if you turn on Key Encryption in Security, and are sent only to the provider they belong to. The update check can be turned off in Preferences. A copy installed from the Microsoft Store is updated by Windows from the Store; its update check only tells you that the Store has a newer version.

Copies built from 24 November 2026 on also have, in Preferences, Offline Mode, which stops every request above, and Check Connection, which, when you press Check Now, tries each of these services once with requests that carry nothing about you or your data. Network Activity, beside them, lists every request made or refused since the app opened.

Feedback You Send

Feedback arrives as an email to the developer. It is kept only as long as needed to reply or to fix what it reports, and it is deleted on request. It is never shared, sold or used for marketing.

What the App Keeps on Your Computer

  • Your preferences, the email address and keys you enter (keys encrypted when Key Encryption is on), and the update check's setting.
  • A small troubleshooting log, which never holds your data or your keys. It leaves your computer only if you choose to send it.
  • After a crash only, the crash's report, kept on your computer and deleted the next time the app starts.

Uninstalling a copy downloaded from this website keeps its settings folder. To remove it as well, delete the medresearch-suite folder inside AppData\Roaming on Windows, inside Application Support on macOS, or inside .config on Linux.

Crashes and Windows

The app sends no crash reports, and it keeps its crashes away from Windows Error Reporting, so Windows does not send Microsoft a report of a MedResearch Suite crash. Depending on your Windows diagnostic data setting, Windows may still tell Microsoft that a program is using a large amount of memory; that notice names the program and its memory use and holds nothing you work with.

The Microsoft Store

If you install MedResearch Suite from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft handles the download, installation and updates under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. Microsoft gives the developer only summary statistics, such as the number of installs and ratings, and any review you choose to post. Where Microsoft offers the developer crash files from Windows, the developer does not download them.

This Website

medresearchsuite.com sets no cookies and runs no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts; its fonts are served from the site itself. It is hosted by Cloudflare, which, like any web host, processes your network address to deliver the pages. Downloads come from GitHub. The demo video is a still picture until you press play; only then does the page load YouTube's player, from youtube-nocookie.com.

Children

MedResearch Suite is a research tool for clinicians, researchers and students. It is not directed to children under 13, and it collects no information about its users of any age.

Laws and Your Institution

Keeping data on your own computer supports the privacy rules that apply to your research, but it does not by itself make a project compliant with HIPAA or any other law or policy. Your institution's rules and review process still apply.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date. A change that sends anything new from your computer is also described in the What's New notes of the update that brings it.
